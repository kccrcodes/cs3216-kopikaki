02 / Voices from the community

We asked,
they shared.

We spoke to seniors in Singapore about their routines, friendships, activities and what makes it harder to meet people.

Come out and enjoy the day together.

Interview 01 · Retired office worker

“Sometimes I want to go hiking or try new food places.”

Mr Tan usually has breakfast with his wife, goes for a walk and sometimes meets friends for coffee. Since retirement, he sees his old colleagues less often because everyone has different schedules.

“I would use an app, but I want to know that the person is genuine first.”

— Mr Tan, 68
Interview 02 · Retired teacher

“A small group would be better.”

Madam Lim meets friends once or twice a week. Many of them are busy looking after their grandchildren. She enjoys gardening, walking and visiting museums.

“Meeting one stranger alone feels a bit awkward.”

— Madam Lim, 70
Interview 03 · Part-time worker

“Show activities near my home.”

Mr Rahman sometimes joins community activities, but many do not interest him. He usually discovers activities through WhatsApp or posters at the community centre.

“If it has too many buttons, I will not know what to do.”

— Mr Rahman, 66
Interview 04 · Lives alone

“I don't want to go shopping alone.”

Madam Wong's children visit on weekends, but she is usually alone during weekdays. She would like to go out for meals and shopping with someone else.

“I would feel safer if the person is verified and we meet somewhere public.”

— Madam Wong, 72
Interview 05 · Retired engineer

“Similar interests matter more than age.”

Mr Lee uses WhatsApp, YouTube and Facebook. He would download another app if it were useful and easy to use.

“I want to meet people nearby who enjoy cycling or coffee.”

— Mr Lee, 67
